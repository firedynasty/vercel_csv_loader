🖨 Print flashcards

8 cards per US Letter sheet, 2 columns × 4 rows. Each card shows its number (#15 = the number you type here to mark it Known). For Duplex, choose Two-sided → Flip on long edge in the print dialog; for Single pages, turn two-sided off. Scale 100%, margins none.

Keyboard shortcuts

These keys always do the same thing. The Click → buttons only change what a mouse click does, so you can hear a card and mark it without switching modes.

Move
Down UpNext / previous card (rows in Table view)
Number keysType a card number (e.g. type 1 then 5) — Enter or a short pause jumps to card #15. It only jumps, so nothing is marked by accident
Listen
SpaceThe whole card — name, then everything after it, read straight through
CommaJust the name (column 2)
PeriodJust the back (column 3 onward)
All three read from the file, so they work even while Front only or Hide answers is hiding the back.
Mark
Enter SlashMark the highlighted card Known — writes x into column 1. Press again to unmark
Answers
LeftTurn Hide answers on / off (switches to Cards view)
RightWith Hide answers on: reveal the highlighted card for 10 seconds
Mouse
Left clickHighlight a card, and do whatever Click → is set to
Right clickWith Hide answers on: reveal that card for 10 seconds
EscClose this box

A one-handed run: Space to hear it, then Enter if you knew it, then Down for the next.
