Tack Cards

Open a spreadsheet — .xlsx or .csv — read it as cards, and tack the rows you already know. A tack writes your mark into column A of the sheet itself, so you can save the file back out and pick up where you left off.

An .xlsx or .csv file. Column A holds your mark (x, ✓, anything short). Column B is the term, and every later non-empty column stacks underneath it as the answer.
